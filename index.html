The essentials

Five habits that matter

Security is less about memorizing rules and more about building a few reliable habits you can use every day.

🔐

Protect your login

Use a unique password or passphrase and enable multi-factor authentication whenever your bank offers it.

Credentials
🎣

Spot phishing

Unexpected urgency, suspicious links, requests for one-time codes, or messages asking you to “verify” can be warning signs.

Scams
📱

Secure your device

Keep your operating system and banking app updated. Use a screen lock and avoid banking on untrusted devices.

Devices
👀

Check before you act

Pause before approving a payment or sharing information. Open your bank app directly instead of following an unexpected link.

Transactions
🚨

React quickly

If you suspect fraud, contact your bank through an official channel immediately and secure your account.

Response
🛡️

Keep alerts on

Transaction alerts can help you notice unusual activity sooner. Review account activity regularly.
